Asking someone for money is a design problem. Do it badly and you lose the gift
and the goodwill. Do it well and you start a relationship that lasts years.

## Reduce the cost of saying yes

The best donation flows ask for one decision at a time. Pick a tier. Pick an
amount. Add your details. Confirm. Each step is small, each step is clear, and at
no point does the person feel trapped.

## Validation as kindness

Inline validation is one of the highest return things you can build. It tells
someone their card has the wrong number of digits the moment they finish typing,
not after they hit submit. It is also an accessibility feature. Error messages
are tied to their fields and announced to assistive tech.

## Say thank you like you mean it

The confirmation screen is not a receipt. It is the first moment of membership.
Make it feel like one.
